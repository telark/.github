<h3 align="center">A protection gate for your Kubernetes applications</h3>

Decide what can change an application, and when.
Telark holds the line during releases and maintenance windows, shows every change that got through,
and tells you why an app broke. Self-hosted, in your cluster.

## Repositories

| Repository | Contents |
|---|---|
| [telark](https://github.com/telark/telark) | The services, Helm charts, CRDs and docs |
| [dashboard-ui](https://github.com/telark/dashboard-ui) | The operator dashboard |

## Get started

- Docs: [docs.telark.io](https://docs.telark.io)
- Install: follow the [quickstart](https://docs.telark.io/getting-started/quickstart). One Helm chart, Kubernetes 1.30+.
